Led By

Mrs. Moore's
Camp Classroom

Camp Kids began in a real classroom in Franklin, Tennessee — and now you can bring your child into that same experience. Debby Moore, the creator and illustrator of the entire Camp Kids world, leads every camp personally.

With 20+ years in early childhood education and a deep passion for EQ-driven learning, Mrs. Moore turns each week into an adventure your child will remember long after summer ends.
