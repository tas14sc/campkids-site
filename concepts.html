The Concepts

Five big ideas at the heart of the Camp Kids way.

Frameworks that turn complicated feelings into something a child can name, hold, and use.

Concept 01

The Physics of Feeling™

Emotions move, and they follow patterns. When kids understand how feelings rise, peak, and pass — like waves on a lake — they stop fearing them and start working with them.

Concept 02

Mindful to Manage™

You can't control what you can't see. We teach kids to notice their feelings first — the small clues in the body and mind — so they can choose their response instead of being swept away by it.

Concept 03

The EQ Cake™

Emotional intelligence isn't one skill — it's a stack. Layer by layer, kids build self-awareness, self-management, empathy, and connection. Every layer matters.

Concept 04

Diver Dave™

Some feelings live deep below the surface. Diver Dave teaches kids how to go gently into the harder emotions — the ones we tend to hide — and come back up with treasure.

Concept 05

Troubles to Treasure™

Every challenge carries something worth keeping. We help kids learn how to look at the hard moments — disappointment, conflict, fear — and find the lesson, the strength, the treasure on the other side.
