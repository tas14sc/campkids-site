The Camp Kids Summer Camp

One week of storytelling, activities, and real EQ tools — led by former WGES and PCES educator Debby Moore.

Where it all happens

Mrs. Moore has spent her career helping kids find their footing in the classroom. Now she's bringing the magic of camp — the storytelling, the imagination, the real-world tools — into one unforgettable week.

Campers meet the whole crew, journey through the four islands of the EQ Treasure Map, and walk away with skills they'll use for the rest of their lives.

The EQ Treasure Map — four islands of learning

The Camp Kids Curriculum

Campers meet the whole crew — including Camper Kallen and Ranger Ross — and journey through four islands, each focused on a key emotional intelligence skill.

Through storytelling, activities, and teamwork, kids leave with real tools for navigating friendships, emotions, and challenges.
